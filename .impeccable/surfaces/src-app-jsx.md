## Scope

Single-page portfolio (src/App.jsx and its components), mode: Persuade. Audience: clients and business owners, recruiters, technical evaluators (see PRODUCT.md). Action: contact via WhatsApp, email or form; download CV. Proof on hand: project facts and live-site captures in src/data/portfolio.js and public/assets/shots; PI Smart stays a labelled illustration. Owner says real screenshots, testimonials and new numbers exist but has not supplied them; slots stay hidden until supplied. Constraints: bilingual EN/ID toggle, nothing invented.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio as a daytime airport terminal: every project is a scheduled departure (destination client, flight code, status LIVE/LANDED), and the last row on the board is the visitor's own team, BOARDING. It refuses the dark dev template with a blue button and a screenshot on the right.

OWN-WORLD: Warm terminal concrete ground, wayfinding-sign yellow (#ffc629) with black type for navigation and primary actions, matte black split-flap boards with charcoal flaps, hinge line and off-white/yellow characters, departure green only for LIVE/now. Barlow (signage grotesk) throughout, Barlow Semi Condensed for display, tabular numerals for every metric. Square corners, sign panels, no soft cards.

STORY: In five seconds the visitor reads the headline, sees the board of real clients flip into place, and finds the yellow check-in action. Then gates (selected work), two check-in counters (build something / hire me), the route (process), the flight log (experience, collapsed rows), the boarding pass (about, CV as the tear-off stub), and the check-in desk (contact).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Yellow sign strip as nav (name, section links with arrows, EN/ID, theme, black "Start a project" block). Headline left at ~5.5rem condensed bold, lead and availability with live WIB clock; right column the black check-in action and CV. Full-width departure board below: header row with live clock, four real rows ending in NEXT: YOUR TEAM, BOARDING, linking to contact.

FORM: Papan Keberangkatan, grounded candidate 6 of 7 (assigned by the roll); seed:e89c9f6e42688e2e. Raises: Miura (EN/ID toggle re-flaps every board at once), forge (tabular numerals on every measured claim), step row (NOW indicator on the flight log), catalogue (dense edge-to-edge gates), ASCII (strict per-character cell grid), fansite (live "updated" clock on the board).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
