## Scope

Single-page portfolio (src/App.jsx and its components), mode: Persuade. Audience: clients and business owners, recruiters, technical evaluators (see PRODUCT.md). Action: contact via WhatsApp, email or form; download CV once supplied. Proof on hand: PISMART and project facts in src/data/portfolio.js; screenshots, photo, CV and testimonials pending from the owner (slots stay hidden until supplied). Constraints: bilingual EN/ID toggle, nothing invented.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A hiring page, not a developer diary. Every section answers "can he build what we need, and how do I start?" It refuses the minimal single-column dev blog with links at the top and contact at the very end.

OWN-WORLD: The category standard at Linear/Vercel/Stripe craft. White ground, slate ink, one confident blue for actions only, Geist throughout, soft-radius surfaces with real offset shadows, product UI as the main imagery, lucide line icons.

STORY: The visitor learns in five seconds who he is, that he is available and for what, sees PISMART as proof of enterprise-scale work, picks their path (build something / hire me), scans selected work and experience, and reaches out without hunting for the button.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky nav with name, section links, EN/ID and theme toggles and a filled "Start a project" button. Left: headline, one-line positioning, two buttons (Start a project primary, WhatsApp secondary), availability line with live WIB time. Right: a large PISMART module illustration panel (labelled as an illustration until real screenshots arrive). Below the fold edge: a quiet row of organisations he has shipped for.
